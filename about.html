About Me

Who I Am

Hello! My name is Emily Oyorsaba, and I am currently earning my associate degree in Multimedia Design at Borough of Manhattan Community College (BMCC), part of the City University of New York. I am in my second-to-last semester and study on BMCC’s Tribeca campus in New York City, where I have been able to work on a wide range of creative and technical projects.

What Holds My Attention

I am interested in design as both a visual and technical practice. I enjoy creating graphics and layouts, but I am also interested in web design, coding, video, and understanding how creative ideas can work across different types of media.

I am also interested in learning more about business and how design connects to professional opportunities. Outside of school, I enjoy sketching, video editing, reading, sewing, and coding.
